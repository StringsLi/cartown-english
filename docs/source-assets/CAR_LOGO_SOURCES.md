# Car Logo Asset Sources

The 50 educational car-badge images are normalized to 512 x 384 transparent PNG files.
Brand marks remain trademarks of their respective owners.

- Primary vector source: [Cardog Icons](https://github.com/cardog-ai/icons), MIT licensed asset tooling.
- Citroen, Peugeot, Renault, and Smart vectors: [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/), CC0-1.0 data and brand-source references.
- Chery, Geely, NIO, XPeng, and Li Auto vectors: Wikimedia Commons files sourced from the manufacturers or simple-logo records.

The application uses these marks only for child-friendly visual identification and pronunciation activities.
